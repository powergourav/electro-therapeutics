# Electro-Therapeutics (1886)

Archive of *A Complete Manual of Electro-Therapeutics, and a Brief Treatise on
Anatomy and Physiology* by **Elizabeth J. French**, 3rd revised edition,
J. B. Lippincott Company, Philadelphia, 1886.

This is a historical medical text. Its claims (including "cranial diagnosis",
the specific effects assigned to each electrical current, and cures for cancer,
tumours and consumption) are **not supported by modern medicine**. Several of
its procedures are unsafe by today's standards. The material is kept here for
historical and reference purposes only, not as medical guidance.
